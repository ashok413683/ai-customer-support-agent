Refund Policy

Customers can request a refund within 7 days of receiving the product.
Refund requests must include the order ID.
Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.


Delivery Policy

Orders are normally delivered within 3-5 business days.
Some remote locations may require additional delivery time.
Customers can contact support if the order is delayed.


Cancellation Policy

Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped.
Once an order has been shipped, it cannot be cancelled.


Support

Customer support is available Monday to Friday.
Support hours are 9 AM to 6 PM.
